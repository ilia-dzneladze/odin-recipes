Khinkali

Description

This is a traditional Georgian Khinkali recipe, a beloved dumpling dish enjoyed across the Caucasus. It is hearty, juicy, and known as the king of Georgian cuisine.

Khinkali may take a little practice to fold correctly, but the process is straightforward. Expect about 1 hour to prepare and cook. Perfect for sharing with friends and family. Portion size: around 15 dumplings.

Ingredients

  • 500g of Flour
  • 250ml of Water
  • 1 tsp of Salt
  • 400g of Ground Beef and Pork Mix
  • 1 Onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 Garlic Cloves (minced)
  • Fresh Parsley and Coriander
  • 1 tsp Black Pepper
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • 200ml Cold Water or Broth (for filling)

Steps

  1. Combine flour, water, and salt into a smooth dough, rest for 30 minutes
  2. Mix meat, onion, garlic, herbs, spices, and cold water to form juicy filling
  3. Roll dough into thin sheets and cut into circles (10-12 cm wide)
  4. Place filling in the center, pleat and twist dough to seal dumpling
  5. Boil salted water in a large pot
  6. Drop dumplings into boiling water, stir gently to prevent sticking
  7. Cook for 10-12 minutes until dumplings rise to surface
  8. Remove with slotted spoon, sprinkle with black pepper
  9. Serve hot and enjoy with hands — hold the top knot and eat carefully!
