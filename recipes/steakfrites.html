Steak Frites

Description

Steak Frites is a French bistro classic, pairing juicy pan-seared steak with crispy golden fries. A simple yet timeless dish enjoyed across Europe.

This recipe is straightforward and quick — around 30 minutes to prepare. Ideal for a satisfying lunch or dinner. Portion size: 1 person.

Ingredients

  • 250g Ribeye or Sirloin Steak
  • 2 Large Potatoes
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 2 Garlic Cloves
  • Fresh Thyme or Rosemary
  • Salt and Black Pepper
  • Vegetable Oil (for frying)

Steps

  1. Peel and cut potatoes into fries, soak in water for 30 minutes
  2. Heat vegetable oil in deep pan, fry potatoes until golden and crispy
  3. Season fries with salt and keep warm
  4. Season steak generously with salt and pepper
  5. Heat olive oil in skillet until hot, sear steak on both sides
  6. Add butter, garlic, and herbs, baste steak until desired doneness
  7. Let steak rest for 5 minutes before slicing
  8. Plate steak alongside fries
  9. Optional: serve with Béarnaise, peppercorn, or aioli sauce
