Carbonara

Description

This is a traditional Carbonara recipe for people to make at Home close to the real thing you can find in Roma. It is a staple in Roman cuisine and holds a special place in my heart and the hearts of all Italians

As to the difficulty of the recipe, it is not too easy to make, but doesn't take too much time. Around 15-20 minutes of attention and 4 main ingredients. If you have a stove, spatula, pan, pot and running water, you will be able to craft this dish! Portion size for 1 person.

Ingredients

  • 80-100g of Pasta
  • 50g of Grated Pecorino
  • 80g of Sliced Guancale
  • 1 Whole Egg + 1 Egg Yolk
  • Black Pepper
  • Salt
  • 500ml of Water

Steps

  1. Add water to pot, put on high heat, add salt to taste, and let it boil
  2. Drop the pasta inside the pot, boil until al dente (time on the box)
  3. Put the sliced guancale on the cold pan and put it on medium heat, stir regularly so the fat renders out
  4. Leave it be for 2 minutes and meanwhile whisk the Egg and Egg Yolk with the Pecorino
  5. After Pasta is al dente, save a cup of pasta water, drain the rest
  6. Turn off the heat on the pan, put pasta in and start incorporating the pasta water with the fat from the Guancale
  7. Once incorporated, add the egg and cheese mixture and mix thoroguhly until sauce like structure is achieved
  8. If the end result is too thick, add water, if its too watery, put on low heat and stir until desired consistency
  9. Plate, add grated Parmesan/Pecorino and black pepper to taste
  10. VOILA! EAT!
